# Amusement Park Ride

**Goal:** To create a mechanical system that transfers energy from a simple motor to different types of rotation or translational movement.

CAD gear ratio, horizontal to veritical translational rotation mechanism, assisted in assembly, and simple electrical assembly consisting of Arduino, safety switch, ultrasonic sensor, H-bridge motor driver and motor.
