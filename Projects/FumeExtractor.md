# 🔑 Solder Fume Extractor (Ongoing)

Intention is to build a Basic Fume Extractor for my own future endeavours with soldering. 

Ideally powered with a 12V plug, a potentiometer + transistor as a variable speed input, and an old pc fan to mimic those cheap retail extractors. 
Will also be using a carbon filter attached. Wanting to keep a modular, easily repairable and swappable design.

Inspired by **[Youtube vid](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8b6f9M_PL8M)**
