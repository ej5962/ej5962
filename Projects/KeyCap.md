# 🔑 Car Key Cap

Wanted to fix some extra car keys that had lost the key caps on the device. CAD on onshape for new design, testing and ultimately building an interference fit two profile keycap that snaps 
on the profile of the car key. Change of profile required prying open car key and gluing back.

Key cap was made using PETG 3D printing. Future improvements include change of material due to rigidity of PETG allowing gap within the car key and the key cap. 
Previous keycaps were originally rubber, installed on the lower profile of the car key, preventing water leakage.
